The source registry contains hashes and retrieval details for downloaded small
resources. The transcript summary records the full-text acquisition. Running
scripts/finalize_source_inventory.py verifies the full Parquet and text hashes,
joins quality flags into the CSV and records the final export/manifest hashes.
Do not use the metadata CSV directly as a labeled train/validation/test manifest.
See docs/study_protocol.txt for cohort, label, temporal and annotation requirements.
